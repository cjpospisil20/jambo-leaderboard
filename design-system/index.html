Contrast rules

Both golds are too light to carry text on paper — tighter than the rust they replaced, so the rule matters more, not less. Gold is a rule-and-fill color only. That constraint is why the points tie keeps its own dark green rather than becoming a gold stamp.

Typography

Three faces. Yellowtail carries the wordmark only. National Park, self-hosted at 400/700/800, is the display face — always uppercase, always solid heavy weight, never outlined. Courier Prime is the body and keeps the score columns aligned.

Body copy is Courier Prime at 1.6 line-height. Monospace keeps the score columns aligned without tabular-figure tricks, and it carries the field-notes register the poster era actually used on signage and permits.

Buttons

Thick outline, zero radius, flat offset block shadow. On hover the block presses into its own shadow — it does not lift, glow or ease.

Unigrid card

Boxy container with prominent vertical rules between columns, after the National Park Service brochure grid. Rules are 3px, never hairlines.

Elevation

Boxy layouts with prominent vertical lines, mimicking classic park brochures and maps.

Season

Zero border radius throughout. The printed look depends on corners staying sharp.

Access

Flat offset block shadow, four pixels down and right, in the primary green. Never blurred.

Band header

Slate blue ground, pale yellow display caps, one gold accent rule beneath. The rule is an inset shadow so it sits inside the outline. On the leaderboard this archetype lives on the table's column-label row, not the masthead — the masthead is transparent so the illustration reads behind it.

Banned

These are not stylistic preferences, they are what separates this system from generic modern UI. Anything on this list is a defect:

  • Pill shapes or any non-zero border-radius
  • Gradients of any kind, linear or radial
  • Blur — filter: blur(), backdrop-filter, soft drop shadows
  • Alpha-soft shadows — shadows are opaque offset blocks
  • Eased transitions on interactive state
  • Outlined display type — -webkit-text-stroke and friends
  • Opacity as a way to express a muted state — use --text-muted
